Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA) has helped many people who came to the United States as children to work and study with greater security. The program has also been shaped by ongoing court cases, so what is available can change over time.

## Renewals

Current recipients generally continue to be able to apply to **renew** their DACA. It is usually recommended to file a renewal well before your current period expires. Our team can help eligible individuals prepare and file renewal requests.

## What DACA does and does not do

DACA may provide temporary protection from removal and, in many cases, work authorization. It does **not** by itself grant lawful permanent residence or a direct path to citizenship.

## Because the rules can change

DACA has been affected by ongoing litigation. Whether new requests are being processed, and other details, can depend on current court rulings. Before taking any action, it is wise to confirm where things stand for your specific situation.
