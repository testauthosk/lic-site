Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Liberty Immigration Council (“LIC,” “we,” “us”) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. We take your privacy seriously — for many of the people we serve, confidentiality is not a preference but a matter of safety. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — your name, email address, preferred language, and anything you choose to share when you submit our Get Help, volunteer, or newsletter forms, or contact us by email.
  • Donation information — donations are processed by our third-party payment processor. We receive your name, contact details, and gift amount; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Technical information — like most websites, we may collect basic analytics data (pages visited, device type, approximate region) to understand how the site is used and improve it.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request for help and assess how we can assist you.
  • To process donations and provide tax receipts.
  • To coordinate volunteer participation.
  • To send updates you have asked for, such as our newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website.

What we never do

  • We never share your information with immigration authorities, law enforcement, or any third party without your explicit written consent, except where disclosure is strictly required by a valid legal order.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or our donor list to anyone.

Donor privacy

Donor information is used solely to process gifts, issue receipts, and — only if you opt in — keep you informed about our work. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law; receipts are provided for every gift.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use a small number of cookies and privacy-respecting analytics to measure visits and improve content. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not build profiles of individual visitors. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to work.

How we protect your information

Form submissions are transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to client information is limited to staff and volunteers who need it to help you, all of whom are bound by confidentiality obligations. Sensitive case details are discussed only after you become a client, under attorney-client confidentiality.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Matters involving minors are handled directly with their parent or legal guardian.

Your choices

  • You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • You may ask us to correct or delete your information, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.
  • You may unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any message or by writing to us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Write to info@libertyimmigrationcouncil.org.

The information on this site is general and not legal advice. Submitting a form does not create an attorney–client relationship.
