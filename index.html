Our purpose

No one should have to face immigration uncertainty alone — without a guide, a language they understand, or someone who treats them with dignity.

Liberty Immigration Council was built for the families the system overlooks — the mother separated from her child, the worker facing removal after decades of contribution, the young person who has known no other home.

We stand beside them when the law feels insurmountable, explain their rights in plain language, and help them find the best path forward.

Answers

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Submit the confidential intake form and we’ll point you in the right direction.

Is my information confidential?+

Yes. Everything you share is confidential. We never share your information with immigration authorities, law enforcement, or third parties without your explicit written consent.

How much do services cost?+

As a nonprofit, we focus on affordability for low-income families — including sliding-scale and pro bono support depending on your situation. We’ll be clear about any costs before you commit.

What kinds of cases do you help with?+

Family immigration, green cards, adjustment of status, citizenship and naturalization, deportation defense, and humanitarian relief. If we’re not the right fit, we’ll refer you to a trusted partner.

What happens after I submit the form?+

You’ll receive an automatic confirmation, and our team reviews your request within 1–2 business days. We then contact you to explain next steps — a consultation or a referral.

Is this legal advice?+

The information on this site is general and not legal advice. An attorney–client relationship is formed only after a formal consultation and written agreement.
